How it works

Matches food to weight. Uses the calories between your first and latest morning weigh-ins. Today's calories are always excluded.

Looks at the trend. Fits a line through all weigh-ins in that period. This is separate from your 7-day average and the Weight page's selected range.

Estimates the energy change. Uses roughly 3,500 kcal per pound gained or lost, then rounds maintenance to the nearest 50 kcal.

Keeps it recent. Uses at most 28 completed calorie days. A missing day breaks the consecutive run; backfilling it restores coverage. The latest weigh-in must be within 3 days. Very rapid weight changes pause the estimate.

Making it useful

Accuracy relies on complete, accurate logging. Include drinks, cooking oils, sauces and snacks. The app cannot tell whether a logged day is complete.

Weigh under similar conditions, ideally in the morning before eating. Keep activity reasonably consistent.

This is a rough estimate, not a precise measurement. Water, glycogen and muscle changes can skew it. The number reflects your recent routine and can change as your activity changes.

Your data stays on this device

Daily saves your foods, calorie logs and weights in this browser or installed app. There is no account, cloud backup or automatic sync between devices. Safari and your Home Screen app may have separate data.

Keep a backup. Clearing website data, uninstalling the app, using private browsing, resetting or losing your device, or browser storage cleanup can remove your history.

Export: tap ⇅ at the bottom right, then Export JSON. Save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. CSV is also available for spreadsheets.

Import: tap ⇅ → Import data and choose a Daily JSON or CSV backup. It merges with your saved data; matching entry IDs and weigh-in dates use the imported values.

Export regularly and before switching devices or clearing app data.
